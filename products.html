The 5000 series cutters allow for the production of a longer tongue and groove. Manufacturers of thicker cabinet doors have found this added length beneficial to produce a stronger joint. Designed specifically to cut a ¼″ thick by 5/8″ deep tongue and groove on material up to 1 1/8″ thick.

The 6000 series stile & cope cutters from Tri-State raise the bar for fit consistency and accurate profile duplication.

The cope cutter comes available in a 1 pc cope; or a 2 pc configuration which allows for tongue thickness adjustment after inserts have been sharpened.

Designed specifically to cut a ¼″ thick by 1/2″ deep tongue & groove on material up to 1 1/8″ thick.

7000 series

Semi-dedicated stile & cope cutter set

Raised panel cutter

Tool diameter
5 3/4″
Bore
1 1/4″
# of wings

The 7000 series “multi-profile” raised panel cutter enables you to cut a multitude of different panel door profiles in the same tool body by simply swapping inserts and their matching “support plates”.

Inserts “locate” (position) in the tool on the cutting edge so that the tongue thickness is always maintained, even when inserts have been sharpened several times.

Hogging cutter

Tool diameter
2 1/2″
Bore
1 1/4″
# of wings
3

The 7000 series hogging cutter makes use of an industry standard: 4 sided indexable carbide knives which are screwed onto the O.D. of the tool. When one side of the knives get dull, loosen the screw and turn the insert to the next, sharp side.

(This tool can also be substituted with our 7000 series rub collar for making arches)

The 7000 series back panel cutter will accept most of todays standard back panel cuts (radius, beveled or square) and like the 7000 series raised panel cutter, the inserts “locate” in the tool on the cutting edge in order to maintain tongue thickness.

Please note that the 7000 series raised panel and back panel inserts are “double-sided”

8000 series

40mm & 60mm wide multi-profile door edge cutter

The 8000 series “multi-profile” cutter allows you to produce many different door edge details while using the same tool body. Available in 40mm and 60mm tool widths, these tools utilize a “support plate” that runs directly behind the insert. These support plates are profiled to match the insert and act as support for the cutting edge.

By simply swapping inserts and their matching support plates, virtually any edge detail imaginable can be produced.

9000 series

Mult-profile door edge, stile & cope cutter

In order to meet the demands of smaller scale cabinet shops, Tri-State developed the 9000 series cutters.

Consisting of a multi-profile door edge cutter along with a semi-dedicated stile and cope cutter set, these tools have smaller diameters and are geared toward the production of ¾″ cabinet doors.

The multi-profile edge tool utilizes a support plate system similar to the 8000 series tools, while the stile & cope cutters are machined with a generically shaped tool body that will accept most popular stile and cope profiles.
