# Research — Tri-State Saw & Tool rebuild

Cleveland, GA shop. Live site fetched 2026-11-23 from https://www.tristatetooling.com/ (home, main1, main2, custom, products, guarantee, contact, about, customers). The certificate is self-signed and expired, so the HTML and images were read with TLS verification disabled. Palette, type, and photos come from those files: black outer page, white content, navy `#000066` / `#000033`, red `#CC0033` / `#CC0000`, Verdana and Arial, steel banner graphics with a magenta drawing-line accent, and the red TRI-STATE wordmark.

## Peer sites

Layout ideas only. No text, images, or branding copied.

### Hancock Tool Co. — https://www.hancocktool.com/

Industrial cutting-tool sharpening and custom tooling (Minnesota).

- Open with a plain service headline, then split the work into a few paths (sharpening, custom, new tooling) instead of one long dump.
- Put named customer comments, with company attribution, high on the page as trust.
- Close with a direct “send us the tools” band and a phone call to action.

### Central Saw Works — https://centralsawworks.com/

Saw blade sharpening and carbide tooling, Spokane, since 1947.

- A thin utility line with years and a real `tel:` link, repeated in the footer with the street address and hours.
- Services as a compact list and short audience tags, not large padded cards.
- One primary action: call the shop or get directions. No invented catalog.

### Union Grove Saw & Knife — https://sawandknife.com/

Custom cutting tools and sharpening since 1983.

- Hero is a short headline, one paragraph, and one button, with the headline under or beside the image rather than buried in it.
- Three service routes side by side (sharpen, sell, custom), each with a text link.
- Product photography sits next to the specification copy for that tool.

## What was borrowed

- Sticky identity bar with the real toll-free number, and a mobile Call / Contact dock.
- Home order: uncovered banner, headline underneath, Google reviews, three service paths, compact chips, then the shop’s own Top 10 and guarantee.
- Standard tooling shown as a photo plus tight spec chips (diameter, bore, wings), not bubble cards.
- Customer comments from the original site stay on Our Customers, attributed as written. They are not presented as Google reviews.
