Custom insert tooling

While we have designed a full line of standard tooling for use in the cabinet industry, most of our production involves designing and manufacturing custom cutters and insert profiles.

To order custom tooling

Please send: .

  • A list of specifications for the tooling you need
  • Type of material and thickness of material you are cutting.
  • Type of machine and specifications such as minimum and maximum diameters; direction and rate of feed
  • A drawing of the profile desired or a wood sample

A computer drawing will be generated of the cutter body(s) and inserts for your approval.
