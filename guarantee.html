Our commitment

At Tri-State, we are committed to outstanding quality not only in our tooling but also in our service. Our goal is to build a partnership with our customers that will enable us to work closely in the development of new ideas and better ways for our customers to build their product.

90 days

Our guarantee

We guarantee all tooling designed and manufactured by this company to be free from defects in workmanship and materials for a period of 90 days. We limit our guarantee to replacement or repair of defective products after an inspection by our engineering department. Defective products must be returned to Tri-State Tooling Technologies in Cleveland, GA.

All insert profiles are guaranteed to be “as ordered”. If upon inspection the insert profile is found to be incorrect, it will be reground or replaced at the discretion of Tri-State at no charge. We do not guarantee against insert knife breakage.
